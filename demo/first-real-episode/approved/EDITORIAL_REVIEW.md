# First Real Episode — Editorial Review

## Decision

The generated run succeeded technically, but the raw outputs were not approved for image generation.

## Raw-output issues corrected

- Replaced awkward Arabic such as `صافير مطر`, `السلك مُصلح`, and `الضوء عائد`.
- Restored the required harmless spark beat that the model changed into a scratch.
- Reduced the structure to four coherent scenes while preserving exactly ten shots and forty seconds.
- Changed every speaking shot to a front or three-quarter, lip-sync-friendly composition.
- Removed character/prompt mismatches where a prompt mentioned a character absent from `shot.characters`.
- Rewrote continuity notes from impossible “visible in every shot” rules to “whenever the character appears”.
- Kept all electrical action non-instructional and visually abstract.

## Approved production structure

- 4 scenes
- 10 shots
- 4.0 seconds per shot
- 40.0 seconds total
- 8 short dialogue lines
- No overlapping dialogue
- One speaker at most per shot
- Final silent hero shot under warm amber light

Publication approval remains not granted until rendered media receives human QC.
