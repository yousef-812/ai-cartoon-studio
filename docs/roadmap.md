# Roadmap

## Foundation

- Monorepo, containers, database, queue, storage, API, dashboard, and CI.
- Series bible, character profile, episode, scene, shot, artifact, job, and approval models.

## First complete episode pipeline

- Story and script agents.
- Shot-list director.
- Character reference workflow.
- Image, video, voice, lip-sync, sound, and render provider adapters.
- Per-shot regeneration and approval.
- Full episode export with subtitles and thumbnail.

## Production hardening

- Cost budgets and provider fallback.
- Automated continuity checks.
- Content safety and commercial-rights records.
- Render recovery and distributed workers.
- Episode analytics and Shorts extraction.
